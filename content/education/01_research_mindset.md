# Research Mindset

Welcome to **MANANZE CRYPTO COMMAND CENTER** education.

## Goals
- Treat crypto research like an operating system: capture questions, sources, and decisions.
- Prefer primary sources (docs, explorers, audits) over social screenshots.
- Separate **DEMO / EXAMPLE** practice data from anything you treat as live.

## Habits
1. One open question per Project Tracker row.
2. Date-stamp every claim.
3. Never paste seed phrases or private keys into tools (MCCC rejects them).
4. When APIs fail, use labelled DEMO tables consciously.

> Not financial advice. Education only.
