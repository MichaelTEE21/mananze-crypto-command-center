# Tokenized Treasuries (Education)

Tokenized treasury products aim to represent short-duration government debt exposure on-chain.

## Research habits
- Prefer official issuer docs and regulatory filings over social posts.
- Treat asset values with measurement timestamps; stale figures must be shown as stale.
- Never call a calculated estimate "TVL".

## Disclosure indicators
- Regulatory status · Custody · Redemption · Attestation cadence

## Warnings
> Education only — not a recommendation to buy or sell any treasury token.
