# Staking

Staking locks tokens to help secure a Proof-of-Stake network (or a liquid-staking derivative protocol).

## Concepts
- **Validator**: node that proposes/attests blocks
- **Delegation**: users stake via a validator or pool
- **Slashing**: penalty for double-signing / downtime (protocol-specific)
- **Liquid staking**: receive a receipt token; adds smart-contract risk

## Research habits
1. Read protocol docs for lockups, unbonding periods, and commission.
2. Separate consensus staking from “staking” marketing on random DeFi farms.
3. Track receipt-token depeg / oracle risk for liquid staking.

## Warnings
- Custodial “stake for me” services may take deposit control — treat as CEX-like risk.
- High APY screenshots without primary sources are speculation, not data.
- MCCC never asks for validator keys or mnemonics.
