# Bitcoin basics

Bitcoin is a decentralized digital money network secured by proof-of-work.

## Key ideas
- **Scarce supply** (21M cap) is a protocol rule — verify assumptions yourself.
- **Self-custody** means you control keys; exchanges hold IOUs when you leave coins there.
- **Block explorers** let you verify transactions without trusting screenshots.

## Research checklist
1. Distinguish BTC the asset vs Bitcoin the network.
2. Prefer primary sources (bitcoin.org docs, BIPs) over influencers.
3. Never share seed phrases — MCCC rejects them by design.
