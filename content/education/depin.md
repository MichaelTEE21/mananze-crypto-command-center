# DePIN

**DePIN** (decentralized physical infrastructure networks) coordinates real-world hardware — wireless, compute, sensors, energy — often with token incentives.

## Diligence focus
1. **Unit economics**: hardware cost, rewards, and realistic utilization — not just emissions schedules
2. **Proof of contribution**: how is work verified? Gaming / sybil resistance?
3. **Supply unlocks** and team allocations (dated tokenomics sources)
4. Separate **network growth metrics** from price speculation

## Warnings
- Hardware + logistics risk is real; online dashboards can lag or be gamed.
- Treat unverified “coverage maps” as SPECULATION until primary methods are clear.
- Never send seeds/keys to “node setup assistants” or AI agents.

> Not financial advice. Education only.
