# Seed phrases (recovery phrases)

A BIP-39 seed phrase derives wallet keys. It is equivalent to full control.

## Absolute rules
1. Write on paper/metal offline — never cloud notes.
2. Never type a seed into a website “support” form.
3. MCCC will **never** ask for your seed phrase.

Phishing often mimics wallet UI. Slow down; verify domains.

> Not financial advice. Education only.
