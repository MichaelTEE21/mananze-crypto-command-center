# On-Chain Basics

## Public vs private
- **Public address**: safe to watch and share for research.
- **Private key / seed**: never enter into MCCC (or any random app).

## Explorers
- Verify contract addresses from official channels.
- Check deploy age, holders, and large transfers.
- For L2s, understand bridge trust assumptions before bridging sizeable funds.

## Practice in MCCC
Use **Wallet Tracking** with `0xDEMO…` addresses or real public addresses only.
