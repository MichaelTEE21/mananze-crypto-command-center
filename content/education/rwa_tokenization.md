# RWA Tokenization Basics

Educational overview of how real-world assets (RWA) can be represented on-chain.

## What tokenization means
Tokenization maps claims on an off-chain asset (or cash-flow) to a ledger token. The token is **not** automatically the legal asset — legal wrappers, custody, and jurisdiction matter.
