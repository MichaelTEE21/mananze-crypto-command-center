# Airdrop Hygiene

## Do
- Track eligibility criteria with dates in **Airdrop Tracker**
- Prefer official docs
- Use separate research wallets (public watch only inside MCCC)

## Don't
- Sign unknown permits
- Share seeds / keys
- Trust DM screenshots of "guaranteed" allocations

Estimates in the tracker default to **DEMO / unknown** until you confirm on-chain.
